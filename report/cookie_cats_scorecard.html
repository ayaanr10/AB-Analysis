Do not ship

Moving the first progression gate from level 30 (gate_30) to level 40 (gate_40) in the mobile game Cookie Cats. Randomised at install; 90,189 players.

At day-7 retention, the change made things worse by 4.3%, a difference too large and too consistent to be chance (p = 0.0016).
